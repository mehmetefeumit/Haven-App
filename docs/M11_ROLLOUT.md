### Decision 1 — Sequencing: prove first, then flip; two-phase via a compile-time build override

**The problem.** The e2e lanes must exercise the LIVE engine, but `liveSyncEnabled` is compile-time and CI runs on committed source — so you cannot prove a flag-flipped build green in CI *without committing the flip*, which defeats "prove before flip." A bare `const` (`= false`, no build-time override) forces flip-and-hope.

**Ruling — introduce a build-time override, keep production OFF, flip in a second commit:**

- **Phase A (author + prove; production still OFF).** Change the const to
  `const liveSyncEnabled = bool.fromEnvironment('HAVEN_LIVE_SYNC', defaultValue: false);`
  This is **still a compile-time constant** (`bool.fromEnvironment` in a `const` context) — with no `--dart-define` it evaluates to `false` and the dark branches tree-shake exactly as today. It is **not** a remote flag (resolved at build time, baked into the binary). The e2e lanes build with `--dart-define=HAVEN_LIVE_SYNC=true` to exercise the LIVE path from the same committed source. Author the M11 e2e scenarios (§4), fold the must-fix hardening (§7), prove green on both platforms. **Production release builds ship no define → OFF.**
- **Phase B (the flip).** Change `defaultValue: false` → `defaultValue: true`. Production now ships the engine. Update `live_sync_provider_test.dart:9` (`expect(liveSyncEnabled, isFalse)` → `isTrue`). Land the SECURITY.md disclosure (§6). Update the WN-plan banner.

**Why `bool.fromEnvironment` over the alternatives:** *bare-const flip on a branch* — CI e2e can't prove the flipped build without the flip commit merged (flipping blind); *sed/patch the const in the CI lane* — source mutation in CI is drift-prone and reviewer-distrusted; *`bool.fromEnvironment`* — idiomatic (mirrors Flutter's `bool.fromEnvironment('dart.vm.product')`), matches Haven's established `HAVEN_E2E_RELAY` dart-define convention, **preserves the tree-shake**, lets ONE commit be tested both ways, and keeps the retained poller path CI-exercisable (build `=false`) for its ≥1-release rollback life. The `enablePeriodicSelfUpdate` const is **untouched**.

> **Persistent receive connection (live-sync engine).** Haven holds a standing WebSocket to your configured circle/inbox relays while the app is in the foreground. A relay learns **that you are online, and which circles you watch, for as long as Haven is open** — a continuous presence signal the previous short-poll model exposed only in bursts; this is irreducible while a live connection is held. Because one connection serves both your circle subscriptions (`#h`) and your invitation inbox (`#p`, keyed to your stable public key), a relay that is in **both** a circle's relay set and your inbox relay set can link your public key to that circle for the session. We minimize exposure by: (a) connecting **only** to relays you configured (never a discovery/default relay; NIP-65 gossip is off); (b) scoping subscriptions to exactly your joined circles and dropping a circle's subscription the moment you leave; (c) closing all subscriptions and the socket on logout; (d) deriving subscription IDs from a **per-session random salt never written to disk**, so a relay cannot link your subscriptions across app sessions; and (e) **disabling NIP-42 authentication** on the receive connection, so your signing identity is never sent to a relay over this socket. The live engine's **notification/decrypt plane is receive-only**; the engine DOES publish convergence auto-commit commits (ephemeral-keyed, per MIP) and resume/catch-up events. It never holds your private Nostr signing key, and never sends key material or your real MLS group identifier over the wire (only the pseudonymous `nostr_group_id`). To also hide your IP/online-presence from the relay operator, run Haven behind a VPN or Tor (e.g. Mullvad).

## REV-1 — Distributed SelfRemove fork (Phase-B blocker, resolved)

> Anchor cited by `haven-core/src/circle/manager.rs:214`/`:3245`/`:3361`/`:7808`/`:7963`+ (the leaver-backstop predicate + the corroboration-gate pre-filter + the acceptance-gate tests). Reachable ONLY with live-sync ON, so it gates the Phase-B flip; the flag-off Phase-A ship is unaffected.

**Status: REV-1 COMPLETE** — fork *safety* proven in Rust, *liveness* backstop wired + tested, all QC folds applied + green, **COMMITTED + PUSHED (`main`@`b2c82ab`, tree clean), flag-off inert.** Verified green: haven-core 901 lib (incl. 9 `rev1_*` + 9 `peek_crypto`) / clippy `-D warnings` / fmt / release; rust_builder 30; Flutter 1659/3.

### The bug (confirmed against code)

Under live-sync, an admin's foreground self-update/remove stages `C_A1`, opens the ~8 s M6 settle window, and **publishes `C_A1` during the window** (load-bearing for two-admin convergence). A peer `SelfRemove` that lands in the window as an order-beating competitor is **trial-applied** by the walk (`converge_commit_collecting_locations`, `haven-core/src/circle/manager.rs`); for an admin receiver MDK `auto_commit_proposal.stage_commit` (`mdk .../messages/proposal.rs:301`) **overwrites `C_A1`'s staged state** (no guard) and `store_pending_proposal`s the SelfRemove. The walk detects the auto-commit (`GroupUpdate{Some}`) and bails `RolledBack` — preventing the *local* phantom-merge — but `C_A1` is already published: passive peers apply it (N+1), MDK `is_better_candidate` keeps the earlier `C_A1` and rejects any re-staged `C_A2`, and OpenMLS forbids re-applying our own commit (`CannotDecryptOwnMessage`/`OwnCommit`). The admin is stranded → **permanent distributed fork**. **MEDIUM-2:** the walk `return`s on the first SelfRemove, so a mixed `[SelfRemove(earliest), RealCommit]` set never adopts the real commit.

**Key deduction:** the destruction is unrecoverable and happens the instant we trial-apply the SelfRemove; two-admin convergence *requires* publishing `C_A1`. So the only correct fix is to **never trial-apply a SelfRemove competitor** — a non-destructive classification BEFORE mutation.

### Classification correction — `content_type`, NOT `wire_format`

The original draft classified by MLS `wire_format` (PublicMessage ⟺ SelfRemove). **That is FALSE and would cause a NEW fork:** RFC 9420 permits **Commits** framed as PublicMessage, and Haven's `MIXED_CIPHERTEXT` groups ACCEPT incoming PublicMessage — so blanket-skipping PublicMessage would drop a real Public commit. **Fix:** after `MlsMessageIn::tls_deserialize_exact`, read `try_into_protocol_message()?.content_type()` — a public, pure, **pre-decryption cleartext** field MDK reads at the same `process.rs:57` routing point before `process_message` at `:66`, so peek and MDK **can never disagree**. Classify: `Proposal ⇒ defer` (skip), `Commit ⇒ real competitor (any wire format)`, `Application ⇒ Location`, parse/decrypt error ⇒ `Unpeekable`. `content_type` strictly dominates `wire_format`; the peek is a PARTITION pre-filter (pull OUT `content_type==Proposal`; the surviving {Commit, Application, Unpeekable} set feeds the UNCHANGED winner walk).

### Why deferred-leave re-injection is dead → the two-driver convergence

The original "buffer the SelfRemove, re-inject it via path-B after `C_A1` merges" is **provably dead**: an MLS proposal is epoch-bound (openmls ValSem003/valn1307), so re-injecting the buffered epoch-N SelfRemove after merging to N+1 is `WrongEpoch(is_commit=false) ⇒ classify_mdk_error ⇒ Other ⇒ silent drop`, never `AutoCommit`. And Haven's leaver wipes its keys after one publish (`completeLeave`, no re-issue). So a race-losing leave becomes a **permanent ghost member** (2-member/sole-committer) or a fresh fork (multi-member) — a forward-secrecy failure (Rule 10) worse than the fork. Skip-the-SelfRemove ALONE achieves the no-fork SAFETY property; the leave-convergence is the LIVENESS half and must land WITH it. The fix converges the leave with a **FRESH-epoch commit** via two drivers:

- **Convergence driver 1 — the redundant non-windowed path (covers almost everything).** A SelfRemove is received by every member; only members with an OPEN settle window for that circle defer it. Any member NOT in a window processes it in regime-1 → MDK auto-commits it, **fully signature-verified** → the leaver is removed → the deferring admin adopts that commit through normal concurrent-commit convergence. This alone converges every case EXCEPT the vanishingly-rare "every member simultaneously in an open window for this circle" (all members running a membership op inside the same ~8 s). *(marmot MEDIUM reframe: driver-1 removes the leaver only if its removal auto-commit WINS the MIP-03 race vs the window-opening non-removal commit; when the non-removal commit wins — needs only ONE pre-windowed member, common — **driver-2 is the PRIMARY converger**, not a rare corner. The admin side has NO auto-backstop, manual remove only — disclosed.)*
- **Convergence driver 2 — the leaver backstop (covers the all-windowed case).** The leaver, after publishing, observes it is still a member on each epoch advance (new `still_a_member` FFI) and re-issues a FRESH `propose_leave` until removed, bounded before `completeLeave` wipes. A fresh SelfRemove is a NEW-epoch Proposal (never a deferred-stale one), so MDK auto-commits it (verified `proposal.rs:442` — any member auto-commits, not admin-gated). The leave-intent is **DURABLE** (`PendingLeaveService` resumes re-issuing on next app-open) so a crashed-then-returned leaver finishes leaving — reuses/extends the leaver-side leave state, NOT a new admin-side table.

### HIGH-1 resolution — the corroboration-gate (owner decision 2026-07-06)

The deferring node **NEVER acts on the peek's unauthenticated `sender()`**. HIGH-1 was: the peek's `PublicMessageIn::sender()` is UNAUTHENTICATED (the FramedContent signature is only verified inside `group.process_message`, which the peek skips; the outer kind:445 ChaCha20 layer is keyed by the group exporter secret every member holds → authenticates "some member," not "which member"). A malicious member could forge `{content_type=Proposal, proposal=SelfRemove, sender=<victim leaf>}`, land it in an admin's window → drive `remove_members([victim])` → evict an innocent non-admin (a regression vs the poller path, which fully processes the forgery → OpenMLS rejects the bad signature). **Owner chose the corroboration-gate:** the peek does exactly one job — classify a competitor by MLS `content_type` and SKIP Proposals (the fork-prevention SAFETY half, which needs no authentication because misclassifying only affects our OWN commit's fate, never a third party's membership). The leave is converged solely by the two VERIFIED drivers above. This **DELETES** Mechanism A (admin fast-path re-issue), the durable `pending_removals` table + drain, the `sender()` leaf-resolution, and the `deferred_removal_pubkeys`/`clear_pending_removal` FFI. HIGH-1 is closed **by construction** (the peek never reads `sender()`); the `Unpeekable`-path forged-SelfRemove guard is defense-in-depth.

### HIGH-2 — fail-safe peek (BLOCKING gates)

A peek decrypt/parse failure MUST be an explicit **NON-destructive SKIP** (classified `Unpeekable ⇒ continue`, never trial-applied) — else, if the peek ever fails to decrypt a SelfRemove that MDK WOULD decrypt (crypto drift; wrong base64/nonce/NIP-44 fallback; or peeking N+1 instead of the pre-merge N), the walk trial-applies it → `stage_commit` destroys `C_A1` → the exact REV-1 fork returns. Re-ranked to BLOCKING acceptance gates: (a) the round-trip decrypt-parity test (peek ⟺ MDK, incl. NIP-44 fallback, at `staged_epoch = N`); (b) the verbatim ChaCha20-Poly1305 routine byte-matches MDK's; (c) epoch-0 (freshly-created, pre-first-message) has a stored secret or is handled. Peek at **`staged_epoch = N` (pre-merge)**, never N+1 (else the peek fails-open into the exact bug). The peek does NOT decrypt the MLS inner ciphertext, so a Location's **coordinates are never exposed** (they stay ratchet-keyed in the PrivateMessage we deliberately do not process). Pass the key by `secret.secret.as_ref()` (no unwrapped copy — `GroupExporterSecret.secret` is `ZeroizeOnDrop`); wrap the decrypted transport bytes in `Zeroizing`. **Group-id leak trap:** never `{:?}`/log a raw `[u8; N]`/`&[u8]` array (derived Debug prints DECIMAL, evading the hex-only `redact_hex_sequences`); `GroupId`/`GroupExporterSecret` Debug themselves emit HEX (`tls_codec-0.4.2` `VLBytes::Debug`) which the ≥16-hex-digit redactor DOES catch — the genuine decimal-evasion vector is a raw array/slice via derived Debug.
